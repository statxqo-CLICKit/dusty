Install on your phone

  1. Open statxqo-clickit.github.io/dusty in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Open Dusty from your home screen. To recycle, open it inside your wallet app's browser (MetaMask: Browser tab).
