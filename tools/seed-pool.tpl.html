Seed the DUSTY/USDC pool

Creates the DUSTY/USDC trading pool on Uniswap v3 (Base) and adds the first liquidity. You sign every step in your own MetaMask. Up to 8 confirmations. Run this after Deploy on Base. The USDC you add goes into the pool, where traders can swap it out for DUSTY; you can withdraw your position later from the Uniswap app.
