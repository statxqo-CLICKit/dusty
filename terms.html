Dusty Terms

Plain-language terms. Last updated November 9, 2026.

1. What Dusty is

Dusty is a tool that lets you send small leftover token balances ("dust") from your own wallet to Dusty and receive DUSTY tokens in return. Dusty runs on smart contracts on the Base network. You connect your own wallet and approve every transaction yourself.

2. What DUSTY is, and is not

  • DUSTY is a utility reward for recycling dust and for future Dusty features.
  • DUSTY has no promised value. We do not promise it can be sold, traded, or exchanged for anything, now or later.
  • DUSTY is not an investment, a share, a security, a loan, or a deposit. Nothing on Dusty is an offer to sell or a request to buy any investment.
  • Do not acquire DUSTY expecting profit.

3. When you recycle

  • The dust you choose is transferred from your wallet to the Dusty treasury. This cannot be undone. Only recycle what you are happy to give up.
  • You receive the DUSTY amount shown in your wallet before you confirm. A 5% recycle fee is taken in DUSTY.
  • You pay the network gas fee in ETH. Dusty never receives your gas.
  • Quotes expire after a few minutes. If a quote expires, nothing is sent; ask for a new one.

4. Your wallet, your keys

  • Dusty never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who asks for them is a scammer.
  • Connecting only shares your public address. Signing in with your wallet is free and moves no funds.
  • You are responsible for your wallet, your device, and checking each transaction before you approve it.

5. Risks

  • Smart contracts can have bugs. The Dusty contracts have been tested but have not had a professional audit.
  • Blockchains, wallets, RPC providers and networks can fail, be slow, or change.
  • Recycling may be paused at any time to protect users, for example if something looks wrong.
  • Laws about digital tokens differ by country and can change. You are responsible for following the laws where you live, including taxes.

6. No advice, no warranty

Nothing on Dusty is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Dusty is provided "as is" and "as available", without warranties of any kind. To the fullest extent the law allows, the Dusty team is not liable for any loss, including lost tokens, lost value, or gas fees.

7. Who can use Dusty

You must be at least 18 and allowed to use crypto tools where you live. Do not use Dusty if you are in a sanctioned country or on a sanctions list, or to move funds from illegal activity.

8. Changes

We may update Dusty and these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. Using Dusty after a change means you accept the new terms.

9. Contact

Questions: tap Ask Dusty in the app for quick help.
