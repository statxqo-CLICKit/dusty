Recycle your dust

Dust is the tiny leftover balance a token leaves behind in your wallet: a few cents of USDC after a swap, a fraction left from a payment. It is often too small to be worth moving on its own, so it just sits there. Dusty turns it into DUSTY on Base.

♻ Open the app▶ Try the demo

Three steps

  1. Connect. Open Dusty and connect the wallet you already use: MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, or any mobile wallet through WalletConnect. On a phone, open Dusty inside your wallet app's browser.
  2. Tick your dust. Dusty reads your balances and lists the dust it can recycle. Today that is small USDC balances on Base; other dust is shown as coming soon. Tick the bits you want to clear.
  3. Confirm and receive DUSTY. Dusty shows the exact DUSTY you will receive, after the fee, before you sign anything. Confirm in your own wallet (the first time, your wallet also asks to approve that exact amount) and DUSTY lands in your wallet.

Good to know

Recycle fee5%, taken in DUSTY. The amount you see before confirming is already after the fee.
How the amount is setPayouts follow the DUSTY/USDC Uniswap pool's 30-minute average rate. The pool is small, so the rate can move a lot.
Network gasYou pay Base gas in ETH, as with any transaction. Dusty never receives it.
Your keysDusty never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Every step is confirmed in your own wallet.
ApprovalsExact amount only, never unlimited.
Audit statusThe contracts are unaudited. Their source is verified on Basescan: see the contracts.
